Hello, I'm Mahi!

A 19 year old multidisciplinary artist and designer from Mumbai who is driven by storytelling, experimentation, and emotional depth. Working across digital media, ceramics, mixed media, and visual design, I create pieces that go beyond aesthetics to explore meaning, perspective, and human experience. My practice is rooted in curiosity and conceptual thinking, often combining layered narratives with visually engaging compositions that invite interpretation and connection.
